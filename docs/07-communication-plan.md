# Communication Plan — Northwind Website Relaunch

## Cadence

| Audience | What | Format | Frequency | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sponsor (Elena Ruiz) | Status, risks, decisions needed | 1:1 meeting + written status report | Weekly | PM |
| Steering group (Sponsor, IT Director) | Milestones, budget, escalations | Steering review | Bi-weekly | PM |
| Delivery team (UX, Eng, QA, Content) | Task status, blockers | Standup | Daily, 15 min | PM |
| Delivery team | Sprint plan and review | Planning + retro | Bi-weekly | PM |
| Warehouse Systems Lead | Integration status, API changes | Sync meeting | Weekly | PM + Eng lead |
| Customer Support Lead | Stock-accuracy readiness, launch impact | Email + review meeting | Monthly, weekly from Week 12 | PM |
| Store Managers (40) | What's changing, when, what to tell customers | Email digest | Monthly, weekly from Week 12 | Marketing liaison |
| Finance | Budget reconciliation | Written report | Monthly | PM |
| All stakeholders | Go/no-go decision | Meeting + written summary | Once, pre-launch | PM |
| All stakeholders | Launch confirmation | Email | Day of launch | PM |

## Escalation path

1. Task-level blocker → raised in daily standup, PM resolves or reassigns within 24 hours.
2. Cross-team blocker (e.g., API access, resourcing) → PM raises directly with the relevant lead within 24 hours.
3. Budget, scope, or timeline risk → PM raises in the next steering review, or immediately by email if it threatens the launch date.
4. Anything threatening the Sep 18 launch date → PM notifies Elena Ruiz directly within 24 hours of identification, not held for the next scheduled update.

## Status report format

Every weekly status report (see [`docs/status-reports/`](status-reports)) follows the same structure so readers can scan it in under a minute: overall RAG status, this week's progress, next week's plan, top risks/issues, budget snapshot, decisions needed. Consistency here matters more than any individual report — stakeholders start pattern-matching after week 2 and read faster.
